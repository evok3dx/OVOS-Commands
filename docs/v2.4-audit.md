## Gate to call this a release

1. Isolated source, fresh-install, upgrade-preservation, rollback and archive checks pass.
2. Reconcile the Brain's **actual** current media/doctor/Whisper source and settings with the candidate. The two chat exports describe operations but do not include those complete installed files.
3. Resolve the repeated Common Play `skill.error` and check live wake word, Qwen command, question, stop, Bella and tray after installation on both machines.
4. Only then mark V3 as a release and update the published release instructions.

log describe intent and constraints. Never silently remove a security requirement
or unresolved item. Move material to `docs/history/` only with its historical
context; flag uncertainty here. Proposed deletions require a separate list and
evidence. Documentation changes accompanying this candidate are identified in
the commit; a broad historical cleanup remains separate.
