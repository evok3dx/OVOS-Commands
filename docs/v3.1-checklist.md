25 September laptop failures. This is a live work list, not a release claim.

implementation or live verification remains; **HISTORICAL** is retained for
context. A passing mock never stands in for speech on Brain or the laptop.

## Decisions and contradictions to keep visible

- Brain's package inventory proves *installed versions*, not active service
  behaviour or that all optional plugins belong on every PC. S01/S07 must
  resolve this explicitly before changing the installer.
- The earlier R04/R08 source review missed two wiring gaps: Qwen's 2× actions
  were absent from its allowlist, and the GUI inventory did not associate
  registered 2× phrases with those actions. Both are corrected in the local
  candidate and covered by the offline check above.
- The [decision log](12-decisions.md) now records V3.1's staged stack migration
  and preserves V3's keep-existing-stack policy as historical. Machine settings
  and local models remain outside the managed virtualenv switch.
- Brain's snapshot has Jarvis dispatcher `2.3.1`; V3 is `3.0.0`, and the new
  package must identify itself as `3.1.0`. The snapshot is a compatibility
  target for OVOS, not a demand to downgrade Jarvis.
- The previous 2.3.1 and V3 procedures stay **HISTORICAL**. No security
  requirement, unresolved issue or decision is silently removed. List any
  proposed deletion with its evidence before changing meaningful docs.
